## How do you start mining Blocknet on Linux?

<Answer>
Download seine, extract it, enter your Blocknet address, and confirm accepted shares on bntpool.com. Start with automatic settings; tuning advice comes after setup.
</Answer>

## Step 1: download the right build

Open the [download section](/#download). Run `uname -m`: choose `seine-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` for `x86_64`, or `seine-linux-aarch64.tar.gz` for `aarch64`. The Linux builds require glibc 2.31 or newer. The x86_64 build supports CPU and NVIDIA; ARM64 supports CPU mining.

<img src="/assets/blog/setup-download.webp" alt="The official download table with Linux and Linux ARM64 archives" loading="lazy" />

## Step 2: extract and launch

Open Terminal. These commands assume the archive is in your Downloads folder; use its actual location if different. On ARM64, replace `seine-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` with `seine-linux-aarch64.tar.gz`. They extract seine into your home folder and launch it:

```sh
mkdir -p "$HOME/seine"
tar -xzf "$HOME/Downloads/seine-linux-x86_64.tar.gz" -C "$HOME/seine"
cd "$HOME/seine"
./seine
```

## Step 3: enter your wallet address

Paste your public Blocknet receiving address or registered `@name`, then press Enter to accept `stratum+tcp://bntpool.com:3333`. The [wallet guide](/blog/blocknet-wallet-for-mining/) shows where to find your address. Seine does not need your recovery phrase or spending keys. It assigns a pool worker name automatically.

Seine chooses threads and tunes automatically. On later starts, launch it from the same folder to reuse `seine-data/seine.config.json`.

## Step 4: check accepted shares

Look for a hashrate and an increasing **Accepted** counter in the [dashboard](/blog/seine-dashboard-explained/). An accepted share confirms the pool received your work; it does not mean a payout has happened. Press `q` or `Ctrl+C` to stop.

Open [My Stats](https://bntpool.com/stats) and enter the same address or registered name to find your worker, shares, and balance.

## What do you need for GPU mining on Linux?

NVIDIA cards need only the NVIDIA driver. AMD cards need the amdgpu driver and the ROCm HIP runtime, as the [AMD guide](https://github.com/zpalmtree/seine/blob/master/docs/guides/amd.md) explains. Seine finds your cards by itself and mines on them alongside the CPU on the x86_64 build. The [NVIDIA guide](/blog/mine-blocknet-nvidia-gpu/) covers choosing cards and checking GPU shares.

## How do you resolve a Linux startup error?

Use the first error message to choose the next check:

| Message or symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| `Exec format error` | Wrong architecture: compare `uname -m` with the archive filename. |
| `GLIBC_... not found` | The binary requires a newer glibc. Use a compatible system or build from source; keep the system C library managed by your distribution. |
| `Permission denied` | Check user execute permission and use a folder on a filesystem that permits execution. |

Once shares are accepted, the [huge-pages guide](/blog/huge-pages-blocknet-mining/) covers optional Linux page setup. The [server guide](/blog/mine-blocknet-on-servers/) covers headless operation and NUMA placement. Neither preparation is required to follow this first-run walkthrough; keep each change separate so you can tell what changed on your machine.
